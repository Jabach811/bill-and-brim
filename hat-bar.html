good questions

Hat bar FAQ

Do I need a reservation?

Nope — walk-ins are fine. Groups of 4 or more should book ahead so we can save enough stools.

How long does it take?

Most hats take about 45–60 minutes from pick to finish.

Can kids do it?

Yes, with an adult along for the ride.

Do I have to know what I want?

Not even a little. That's what Kim is for — she'll help you find your style at the bar.

Can I bring my own hat to restyle?

Yes — ask about restyle pricing in the shop.
