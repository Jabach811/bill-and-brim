the fine print, plainly

Party questions

How far ahead should we book?

Two to three weeks is usually plenty. Give yourself more runway for weekend dates near the holidays — those go first.

What does the deposit cover?

The $100 deposit holds your date and counts toward your total. It's refundable up to 7 days before your party.

Can we party after shop hours?

Yes — that's how most parties happen. Private groups book around normal shop hours, and Sundays are open for buyouts. Current hours are on the Visit page.

What if someone doesn't want a hat?

They can come hang out. Spectators are free — plenty of people show up for the company and end up at the bar anyway.

Do you travel?

Ask us about pop-ups for large events. We'll tell you what's possible.

Still not sure? Just ask — we're friendly.
