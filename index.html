wait — what's a hat bar?

It's the most fun you'll have in a hat.

Think of it like a build-your-own-bouquet bar, but for hats. You pick the hat, then work down the bar choosing everything that goes on it. No two ever come out the same — that's the point.

1

Pick your hat

Trucker, rancher, wool felt, wide brim — try them all on until one feels like you.

2

Style the brim

Bands, feathers, charms, dried florals, playing cards, custom branding — build it piece by piece at the bar.

3

Wear it home

We finish it right there while you watch. You walk out the door in a one-of-one.
