good to know

Visit FAQ

Do I need an appointment?

Nope — walk-ins are welcome during shop hours. Groups of 4 or more should book ahead so we can save enough stools.

Are you open Sundays?

The shop's closed Sundays and Mondays, but private parties can still book for a Sunday.

Where do I park?

Free street parking right out front.

How do I reach you?

Call or text us at (209) 679-8076 — that's the fastest way. An Instagram DM to @bill_and_brim works too.
